About BEUMER Group

BEUMER Group is a leading international provider of conveying and loading systems, palletizing and packaging technology, as well as sorting and distribution systems. With over 6,000 employees worldwide, the company develops and manufactures high-quality intralogistics solutions for a wide range of industries.

Founded in 1935, BEUMER Group has established itself as an innovation leader in the field of intralogistics, offering customized solutions that optimize material flow and increase operational efficiency. The company's portfolio includes bagging machines, palletizing systems, conveyors, and complete system solutions for airports and parcel handling.

Headquartered in Beckum, Germany, BEUMER Group operates globally with subsidiaries and representatives in more than 70 countries, ensuring close proximity to customers and providing comprehensive service and support worldwide.

Application Overview

FillPac Vision Intelligence Platform is a real-time production vision system built for BEUMER Group's FillPac bagging lines. It uses camera-based AI inspection to count bags as they move through the packing process, verify that each bag has been correctly printed, and flag any bags where print is missing.

The platform continuously monitors up to four production cameras, tracking per-camera bag counts, print quality, and frames-per-second performance, while also watching conveyor motion to detect slowdowns, warnings, and confirmed jams before they impact throughput. Operators can review live camera feeds, production statistics over customizable time ranges, and system health for the PLC, PMS, and DCS integrations — all from a single dashboard, with data backed by persisted production events for reporting and analysis.
